# **INF6027 Project Page**
## **🏗️ Brief Introduction**  
This study analyzed the risk of Food access in different regions, using cluster analysis to classify regions into high and low risk groups based on seven key indicators of the Priority Places for Food Index (PPFI).Through logistic regression model, we find that factors such as supermarket accessibility have a significant impact on high risk prediction.

## **❓ Research Questions**  
- Can different regions be classified according to food availability and socio-economic characteristics?  
- Based on the seven indicators of the Food Priority Areas Index, which factors have a significant impact on a region becoming a high-risk area for food access.

## **🔍 Key Findings** 
- Based on food availability and factors associated with social disorders, cluster analysis can be used to classify high - and low-risk groups.  
- The proximity of supermarket facilities, supermarket accessibility, e-commerce availability, socio-demographic characteristics and accessibility of non-supermarket supply places have significant effects on the prediction of high-risk areas  
- Fuel poverty and food support have less impact on risk projections
